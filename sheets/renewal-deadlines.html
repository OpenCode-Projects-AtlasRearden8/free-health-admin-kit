Paperwork has a habit of expiring quietly. This page is the anti-surprise: file it where you plan bills or keep the good pens. When a deadline passes a row, fill in the "done" column and breathe a little easier.

The ground rules

Health insurance renewals usually land on December 31 / January 1, with an open-enrollment window in the fall; prescriptions usually renew any time at the pharmacy but authorizations often expire a year to the day after approval. When in doubt, write the earliest date you know.
