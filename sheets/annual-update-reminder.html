The once-a-year sweep

  • Print a fresh copy of every sheet in this kit (build script makes all 6 PDFs)
  • Refill the medication wallet card — dose changes and stoppeds included
  • Copy insurance card front and back info into the call script page
  • Check every row of the renewal-deadlines tracker; add new dates
  • Verify primary care + pharmacy contact info is still right
  • Archive last year's EOBs and appeal letters to a folder or box
  • Confirm forms/prep sheet info for any upcoming appointments
  • Empty the "ask doctor" card and keep only unanswered questions
  • Skim one phone-call script line: "send it to me in writing"
  • Shred anything with more personal detail than you need to keep
  • Pat yourself on the back — this is real, boring, heroic work

Happy crumbs (optional, but why not)

Same-day treats make yearly chores stick: a favorite drink while you sort, one song per folder, or a sticker for each finished sheet. Low-energy day? Do one checkbox this week and one next. The pile only ever shrinks.
