Medication List Wallet Card

Free Health Admin Kit
Blank by design — you fill it in

A tiny card pharmacists, ERs, and new providers are always glad to see. Print this page, fill in a card with what you know, then cut it out: cut along the solid outline and fold on the dashed middle line so the medication list lives on the inside. Start with the guide below, keep the master list somewhere safe, and make a fresh card whenever something changes.

Guide: write these before cutting

  • Each medication (nickname ok: "bp pill")
  • Dose & when taken
  • Why you take it — plain words welcome
  • Allergies, if any
  • Prescriber / pharmacy phone
  • Insurance company & member ID
  • Your emergency contact
  • Mark anything you must never skip
