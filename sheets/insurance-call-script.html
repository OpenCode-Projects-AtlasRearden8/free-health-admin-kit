Insurance Phone Call Script

Free Health Admin Kit
Administrative help only — not medical or legal advice

Calling the insurance company can feel like a maze with hold music. This script gives you the exact words and a place to write the answers, so you never have to improvise. Fill in your details, keep it by the phone, and take your time — you're allowed to slow them down too.

Before you dial

  • Insurance company: Member ID:
  • Phone number on the back of the card:
  • Why I'm calling (one sentence):
  • Paper, pen & this script in front of me
The call. “Hi, my name is , member ID . I'm calling about . Can you please explain what my coverage is, and walk me through the next step?”

Handy phrases (pick as needed)

  • “Can you please send me that in writing, or point me to it in my plan documents?”
  • “Can you tell me the reference number for this call?”
  • “If a claim is denied, what exactly do I write in my appeal?”
  • “Can I speak to someone who handles this kind of request?”
  • “Is that a copay, coinsurance, or deductible amount — and how much have I already paid toward my deductible?”
