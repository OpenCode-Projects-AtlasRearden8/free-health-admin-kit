Question List Card

Free Health Admin Kit
Appointment prep only — not medical advice

Questions flock in at the worst times and vanish by the visit. Print this card and put copies in your bag, on the fridge, and by the bed. Jot things down whenever they pop up; bring the card to appointments and read from the top. Cut the page in half for two cards.

Questions for:

Ask your top one or two questions first — visits are short. It is always okay to ask "can you say that in plain language?"

Questions for:

Mark a star next to questions that matter most. Circle ones already answered and happily ignore them next time.
