Appointment Prep Sheet

Free Health Admin Kit
Appointment prep only — not medical advice

Print one of these a few days before an appointment and fill it in when you have a few spare minutes. Checking boxes now means less brain-juggling at the front desk later.

1. Who, when, where

  • Clinic / office name:
  • Date & time:   Address:
  • Reason for this visit:
  • Provider I'll be seeing:
  • Getting there (driver, bus, parking note):
  • Arrive how early?

2. Call ahead about

  • Forms to fill out or bring (ask online? portal? mail?)
  • Insurance referral or prior authorization needed?
  • Cost estimate, copay, or payment plan question
  • Interpreter / assistance needed
  • Running late that day? Best number to call:

3. Pack into the bag / folder

  • Photo ID
  • Insurance card(s)
  • Medication list (wallet card works!)
  • List of allergies
  • Copay / card / cash
  • Past records or imaging CDs
  • This filled-in sheet
  • My questions list
  • Notebook or phone for notes
  • Water & a small snack

4. Night before

  • Fasting needed?
  • Taking medication as usual, or hold?
  • Logbook / readings / diary appended below
  • Bag packed by the door

5. Quick notes for the visit itself
